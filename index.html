Wind-tunnel curves reveal a propeller’s twist

We hid each of 27 commercial APC propellers in turn and rebuilt its blade from its measured thrust and efficiency curves alone. Twist came back within 0.62° (median), closer than the best same-diameter catalogue blade for 27 of 27.

What the curves can’t tell you

Chord. Thrust and efficiency barely change when a blade gets a little wider, so the test does not contain that information. The recovered c/R beats the same-diameter blade in only 3 of 27 cases. The planform is better taken from the product family.

What comes next

The journal paper adds a niching search that returns every equally good blade, tolerances set by the surrogate’s own uncertainty, and independent checks of the recovered geometry.
